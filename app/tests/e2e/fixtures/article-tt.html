Fixture trusted types

This page lets no script write markup: it enforces Trusted Types and allows no policy, so that code which parses HTML strings fails here, as it does on sites that protect themselves this way. The reader view only reads the elements of the page.

The second paragraph is long enough for the check of the reader view, which needs a few blocks of text of at least one hundred and forty characters before it offers its button next to the address bar of the browser.

The third paragraph closes the page, with one more sentence of plain text that the reader view shows in its own document, in the colors and the fonts of the browser.
