Fixture cases of articles

The first paragraph of the cases tells of a small town by the sea, where the fishing boats leave before the sun comes up, and where the market opens once they are back, with the catch of the day on long tables and the gulls waiting on the roofs around the square.

The second paragraph goes on with the day: the nets dry in the wind, the children run to school along the harbor wall, and the old men sit on the bench by the church, where they talk about the weather of tomorrow and the weather of the years that have gone by.
