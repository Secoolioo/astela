# History

> **Status:** available from 0.1.0-beta.3.

Astela keeps a history of the web pages you open, on your computer only. To see it, open the menu at the right end of the toolbar and choose **History**, or press Ctrl+H (Command+Y on macOS).

The history page lists the pages by day, newest first. Type in the search field to find pages by words of their title or address. Click a page to open it, or Ctrl-click (Command-click on macOS) to open it in a new tab. The × button removes a page from the history, and **Clear history** removes all of it.
