# History

> **Status:** available from 0.1.0-beta.3.

Astela keeps a history of the web pages you open, on your computer only. To see it, open the menu at the right end of the toolbar and choose **History**, or press Ctrl+H (Command+Y on macOS).

The history page lists the pages by day, newest first. Type in the search field to find pages by words of their title or address. Click a page to open it, or Ctrl-click (Command-click on macOS) to open it in a new tab. The × button removes a page from the history, and **Clear history** removes all of it. From 0.1.0-beta.7, Clear history also clears the back and forward lists of your open tabs, also of tabs that sleep, and of the session that Astela keeps for the next start, together with its backup copy: each tab keeps only the page it shows.
