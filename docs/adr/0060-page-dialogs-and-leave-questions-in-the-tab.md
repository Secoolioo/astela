# 0060. Page dialogs and leave questions in the tab

## Status

Accepted. Amends [ADR 0034](0034-overlay-view-for-popovers-and-prompts.md) with a question over a part of the window, which takes the focus and follows that part instead of closing, and [ADR 0053](0053-sleeping-tabs-and-an-exact-session.md), whose rule that the browser never asks a page now holds for the closes that the browser makes itself: a tab that people close asks its page, when they typed into it. Built in 0.1.0-beta.7, after the early test of the newest build.

## Date

2026-11-15

## Context

The early test of the build with picture in picture found three ways in which a page could hold people or lose their text:

- `alert()` and `confirm()` went to Electron's own handler, the internal event `-run-dialog` of each `WebContents`. It shows a box of the system without a title for the page's window, which disables the whole `BaseWindow`: the tab strip, the window's controls, which the browser draws ([ADR 0048](0048-title-bar-drawn-by-the-browser.md)), and every shortcut. The box never names the site, shows over the active tab when a tab in the background asks, stays after its tab closed, and offers no way out of a loop, as Electron adds its check box only with `safeDialogs`. A page that calls `alert()` in a loop, as tech support scams do, left no way out with the mouse or the keyboard, and the session brought it back after a kill.
- `prompt()` threw `prompt() is not supported.`, which Electron's renderer puts in its place, in the top frame and, as the web views have `nodeIntegrationInSubFrames`, in every iframe.
- A page that asks to stay, with `beforeunload`, as editors with changes that are not saved do, got no answer to Electron's `will-prevent-unload`: Electron then cancels the navigation without a word. Enter in the address bar, F5, Reload, Back and the page's own links did nothing. Closing its tab, on the other hand, never asked the page ([ADR 0053](0053-sleeping-tabs-and-an-exact-session.md)), and reopening the tab brought it back without its history and its text.

## Decision

- **Dialogs leave Electron:** the code for the requests of web pages, which loads before the first web page, takes `alert()` and `confirm()` of every page of a web session, those that exist then and every later one, also those that pages open (`watchPageQuestions` in `app/src/main/page-questions.ts`): it removes Electron's handler of `-run-dialog` and handles the event itself. Electron registers exactly one handler for each page; when the count differs, as after an update of Electron that changed the event, the page keeps Electron's handler, the log records an error, and the web views have `safeDialogs`, so that Electron's box offers to stop the dialogs of a site. An end-to-end test fails when the browser's handler is not the only one of a new page.
- **`prompt()` through the frame preload:** the frame preload puts a `prompt()` of its own into the page's world with `contextBridge.executeInMainWorld`, once and again on its `document-start`, as Electron sets its own again in an iframe after the preload ran. It asks the browser process with a synchronous message, `page.prompt`, which always gets an answer: the text, or `null` for Cancel, for a closed tab, a navigation, or a sender that is no page of a web session (`app/src/preload/frame.ts`, `app/src/main/web-frames.ts`). Documents without a preload, such as an iframe of `about:blank` and the first empty document of a popup, keep Electron's `prompt()`, which throws.
- **The question in the tab:** each dialog waits in a queue of its page (`DialogQueue`, `app/src/domain/dialogs/dialog-queue.ts`) and shows in the window's overlay, over the page of its tab or its side of a split view alone, as a question of the kind `dialog` (`app/src/main/page-dialogs.ts`). The overlay lies over that area only (`sheet` of `WindowOverlay.open`), takes the focus as a question over the whole window does, and follows the area when the window lays its views out anew, instead of closing; the tab strip, the window's controls and the shortcuts stay with people. The question stays while the window runs a shortcut, so that the address bar or the find bar takes the focus while the question waits over the page, as Chromium leaves a dialog of a tab up while the address bar takes the focus, and it stays while people only move to the other side of its split view (`overPage` of the overlay). A question that shows again, after its tab showed again or a menu over it closed, takes the focus only when its page has it (`focus` of `WindowOverlay.open`), and leaves it where people work otherwise, such as in the other side of a split view. A question that opens while its window has no focus, as when people work in another app, gets the focus with the window, so that Enter and Escape answer it. The question names the frame that asks: _127.0.0.1:8080 says_, or _An embedded page at localhost:8080 says_ for a frame of another origin than its page, or _This page says_ without a host, as for a file. It shows the message as plain text, its first 2,048 characters, and OK, with Cancel for `confirm()` and `prompt()`, whose field starts with the text that the page gives and is chosen, so typing takes its place. OK has the focus, and Enter answers, as in other browsers; Escape and Cancel refuse; a click next to the question answers nothing.
- **A tab in the background holds no page:** a page waits for its answer, and with it every page of its process, such as the tab that opened it and shows in front. As in Chromium, a page whose tab does not show gets OK for `alert()` at once, and its message shows once the tab shows; the page's next dialog takes the place of such a message, so only its last one waits to show. `confirm()` and `prompt()` get Cancel at once, and nothing of them shows.
- **A question waits:** a question that showed hides and waits while its tab does not show, and shows again once its tab shows. It never takes the place of what the overlay shows, such as a menu, the command palette or a popover that people opened over it: it waits until that has closed (`onDidClose` of the overlay), and none of them answers it. The command palette does not take the place of a dialog, as of the other questions of sites ([ADR 0052](0052-command-palette-and-tab-search-in-the-overlay.md)).
- **Every dialog gets one answer:** Cancel when the page's main frame starts to load another document, when the document changed, when its tab closes and when its renderer is gone. A frame that asks again while its question waits got its answer elsewhere, such as from developer tools, and its question before goes.
- **Stopping a site:** from the second dialog of a page in a document on, whichever of its frames asks, the question offers _Don't let this site show more dialogs_. Once it is ticked, the page gets Cancel at once for its dialogs, also for those of its embedded pages, until its tab loads another document. A loop ends at once then, and the page goes on running. As Chromium keys this on the page and not on the frame that asks, a page whose embedded pages ask one dialog each from ever new origins gets the offer as well.
- **No hang question for a page that waits:** while a dialog of a page waits for its answer, the page cannot run; the question whether to wait for a page that stopped responding does not ask about it (`app/src/main/hang-prompt.ts`).
- **The question to stay:** every page of a web session answers `will-prevent-unload` at once with a box of the system that waits for its answer (`askToLeave` in `app/src/main/leave-prompt.ts`), as Chromium reads the answer as soon as the event returns: _Leave 127.0.0.1:8080?_ with **Leave** and **Stay** before people leave a page, _Reload 127.0.0.1:8080?_ with **Reload** and **Stay** before F5, Reload and Reload without cache, and _Leave 127.0.0.1:8080?_ with **Leave** and **Cancel** before a tab closes; each says _Changes you made may not be saved._ Chromium asks only for a page that people used, so a page that nobody touched leaves without a question. The reload and the close note their reason for the question beforehand (`app/src/platform/electron/unload.ts`). The code loads with the code for web pages, not on the start-up path.
- **Closing a tab asks its page, when people typed into it:** the close button, a middle click, Ctrl+W or Command+W and **Close** in the menu of a tab run the tab action `close` of the window (`askToClose` in `app/src/platform/electron/window-commands.ts`). A web page that people typed into since it loaded its document (`TabActivity.typed`) is closed with `close({ waitForBeforeUnload: true })`, and the tab stays until the page agrees: a page that asks to stay asks the question above; Leave closes the tab through its view, and Cancel keeps it. Any other page closes at once, as before. The browser's own closes stay silent, as [ADR 0053](0053-sleeping-tabs-and-an-exact-session.md) decided: a tab that sleeps, a view that another one replaces, closing several tabs, a group, a window or the browser.
- **A closed tab keeps its history:** `TabService.close` reads the tab's history with what its pages hold now, within the limits of a session (`trimHistory` with `sessionLimits`: 24 entries back and forward, 32 KiB of page state per entry and 128 KiB per tab), and the strip keeps it with the address, also for the tabs of a group that closed (`KeptTab` in `app/src/domain/tabs/tab-strip.ts`). Reopening the tab brings back its back and forward history and what its page held, such as typed text, the way a tab wakes. A tab that closes after its page asked to stay keeps the history of the moment of the answer. Clearing the whole history forgets these histories; the closed tabs keep their addresses. They stay in memory with their window, also in private windows, and never reach a file.

### Deviations from the plan

- The plan numbered this decision 0059, which the record of site information took.
- The plan takes the dialogs in `guardWebContents` (`session-security.ts`), wires the leave question to `web-pages.ts` through an option of the windows that `bootstrap.ts` gives, and adds `closeTab` to the window; all of these but `web-pages.ts` are on the start-up path. The questions are taken by the code for the requests of web pages, which loads before the first web page and takes the pages that exist then, and the close goes through the tab actions of the window commands, so the start-up path grows by 258 bytes instead of more than 600.
- The plan names an embedded page when its site differs from the page's; the browser compares origins, as Chromium does.
- `safeDialogs` comes without a message of the browser's own: the catalogs of the browser process are not on the start-up path, so Electron's own English text shows in the case that should not happen, when the browser could not take a page's dialogs.
- The plan lets the question of a tab in the background wait until its tab shows. That would hold the page and every page of its process, such as the tab in front, with a question that nobody sees, so the browser answers as Chromium does: OK for an alert, whose message shows later, and Cancel for the others. Chromium also takes a tab of a window other than the one used last for one in the background; the browser looks at the tab in its own window, where the question shows. Chromium marks the tab of such an alert and logs a warning in the console of a page whose `confirm()` or `prompt()` it cancelled; the browser does neither.
- The plan counts the dialogs and stops them per origin in a page; the browser counts and stops them per page, as Chromium does, so that frames of ever new origins cannot avoid the offer.
- As the plan says, a dialog ends when the main frame starts to load another document. Chrome also ends it as soon as people ask for another page in the tab, before the page's `beforeunload` ran; Electron reports no such moment, so a navigation that people start while a dialog waits starts only once the page could answer its `beforeunload`.

## Consequences

Positive:

- A page that asks in a loop no longer holds the window: people close its tab, switch tabs or stop its dialogs, also after a restart that brought it back.
- Dialogs name the site that asks, show only over their own tab and go with it. A tab in the background never holds its process with a question that nobody sees, and a question stays while people use the address bar or the other side of a split view.
- `prompt()` works in pages and in their iframes, as intranet apps expect.
- Leaving, reloading and closing a page with unsaved text ask first, as in other browsers, and a closed tab comes back with its text and its back history.

Negative:

- `-run-dialog` is an internal event of Electron, which an update can change; the end-to-end test and the fallback with `safeDialogs` cover it.
- A `confirm()` or `prompt()` of a tab in the background gets Cancel without a word to people or to the page's developers, as in Chromium but without its warning in the console.
- The question to stay is a box of the system that holds the browser process while it shows, in every window. A question in the overlay needs a cancel and a retry of the navigation, which links and scripts of a page cannot have; it is planned as a later refinement.
- After Cancel in the question to stay of a closing tab, the page holds a transient user activation for a few seconds, which Electron gives it before it asks; only pages that people typed into get it.
- Typing in the last second before a close may be missing when the tab opens again: Chromium keeps the state of a page about a second after it changed.
- A closed tab keeps up to 128 KiB of page states in memory, for at most 25 tabs of each strip.

## Alternatives considered

- **`safeDialogs` alone:** its check box stops a loop, but the box stays modal to the window, nameless and after its tab; pages that pages open keep `safeDialogs` off.
- **A `prompt()`, `alert()` and `confirm()` of the frame preload for every dialog:** it needs more code, and a fresh frame without a preload would keep Electron's handler.
- **A second view per window for the questions of pages:** menus could show over it, but each window would carry another page, and the focus, the order of the views and the keys would need a second owner.
- **A question in the overlay that cancels the navigation and starts it again after Leave:** links and scripts of a page give the browser no target to start again.
- **Asking before closing every tab whose page asks to stay:** Electron would give a page that nobody touched a gesture that no person made.
- **Asking, without the page, before closing a tab that people typed into:** it would also ask for every search field and for editors that saved already.
