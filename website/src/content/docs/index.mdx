A desktop browser for Windows, macOS and Linux, built on Chromium through Electron. Start-up
time and memory stay within budgets that every change is measured against. There is no
telemetry, no ads, no sponsored tiles, no news feed and no account.

## In place

- Tabs at the top, at the bottom or at either side, find in page, zoom per site, full screen,
  downloads, site permissions, popups and private windows; a page that cannot load says why.
- History with search, bookmarks with a bar, session restore, and the import of bookmarks,
  history and open tabs from other browsers.
- A content blocker with the default filter lists of uBlock Origin, on from the first start, with
  a switch per site.
- A settings page with search, themes and colors of your own, density, text size and fonts, a
  toolbar and a new tab page to arrange, search engines with keywords and `!bangs`, and keyboard
  shortcuts of your own.
- Tabs with the icons of their pages that animate, can be dragged, pinned and muted and have a
  menu of their own; an address bar that shows the site first with the security of its
  connection and suggests open tabs, bookmarks and visited pages; window controls in the top row
  of the browser.
- HTTPS-First, DNS over HTTPS, blocked third-party cookies and clearing browsing data.
- Packages for Windows (NSIS installer, MSI), macOS (disk image) and Linux (AppImage, deb, rpm),
  for x64 and arm64. Every format is installed and started in CI on every change.
- Signed updates with delta downloads. After two failed starts, the previous version comes back
  on its own. deb and rpm packages update through APT and DNF repositories.
- Policies from the Windows registry, macOS configuration profiles and JSON files on Linux, read
  before the first window opens.
- Budgets for start-up time, memory, CPU and bundle size, measured against a bare Electron app on
  all three systems for every change. See [performance](docs/architecture/performance/).
- Security fixes from Electron ship in a patch release within 72 hours, critical ones within
  24 hours.

## New on main

On `main` now, and in the next beta, 0.1.0-beta.7:

- **Dark mode:** calmer lines and evener surfaces, mixed from the colors of the theme.

## Planned for 0.1.0

- Layout presets such as a compact and a zen layout, site information in the address bar and browser
  menus in an overlay.
- A command palette, tab search, sleeping tabs, split view, tab groups, workspaces, a reader view
  and picture-in-picture.
- Saved passwords and autofill.
